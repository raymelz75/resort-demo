Welcome

A quiet estate retreat in the Shevaroys

Misty Hills is a small family-run resort set inside a coffee and pepper estate, a few minutes from Yercaud Lake. Mornings start with mist over the valley and fresh estate coffee; evenings end around the campfire under a sky full of stars.

Whether it's a honeymoon, a family weekend or a team offsite, we keep things simple, warm and personal.

Location

How to reach us

Misty Hills is in Yercaud, in the Shevaroy Hills above Salem, Tamil Nadu.

  • 🚗 From Salem: about 30 km, around 1 hour up the ghat road with 20 hairpin bends
  • 🛣️ From Bengaluru: about 230 km, around 5 hours
  • 🛣️ From Chennai: about 360 km, around 7 hours
  • 🚆 Nearest railway station: Salem Junction
FAQ

Good to know

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12 noon and check-out is by 11 AM. Early check-in is possible on request.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A South Indian and continental breakfast is included with every room.

Is there parking at the resort?

Yes, free parking is available for cars and bikes inside the property.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome in the Valley View Cottages. Please tell us when you send your booking request.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. After that, one night's charge applies.
